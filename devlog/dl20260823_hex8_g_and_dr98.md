## Summary

HEX8's gradient is the first with genuinely different structure from the curl:
G = s[∇F ⊗ ∇ξₐ + F·H⁽ᵃ⁾], where H⁽ᵃ⁾ is the inverse-map Hessian —
symmetric, hence invisible to the curl (whose "Hessian cancellation" is why
C() never needed it), zero on affine hexes, and computed as
H⁽ᵃ⁾ = −A·Cm·Aᵀ from the trilinear map's mixed second derivatives (reused
from the tested Lagrange d2NdXi2, Voigt rows 5/4/3). The derivation was
numerically probe-verified BEFORE the audit round (formula vs FD through a
Newton-inverted map: 1.4e-11).

The C.1 round surfaced DR-98: **EF_HEX8::C() returned the negated curl** —
against the base contract, all four previously audited elements, and its own
sibling EF_HEX8TS (identical mEx/mEy/mEz semantics, opposite operand order).
Found by Codex statically, verified by Claude's probe with two independent
methods (dev 0.0 vs −curl / 0.34 vs +curl; FD cross-check |code+FD| = 6e-12),
invisible historically because HEX8 had zero battery coverage and K = CᵀC is
sign-invariant. Christian approved the fix ("We have never used HEX8 in
depth… Let's fix it"); the operand swap landed with a DR-98 comment, and the
new battery closes it permanently: the Stokes-consistency test integrates
BOTH sides (loop circulation vs C_z face flux) because a curl tie alone
passes when E and C are negated together. With the old sign deliberately
restored, Stokes fails loop = 1 / flux = −1. No consumer carried a
compensating sign (both auditors searched): the postprocessed j on HEX8
blocks is corrected, not re-broken.
