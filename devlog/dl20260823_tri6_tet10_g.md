## Summary

TRI6 (8 dofs) and TET10 (20 dofs, edge + orientation-combined face dofs)
compute their curls from reference partials with the nabla vectors held
fixed — correct only because the omitted ∂(∇λ)/∂x terms are symmetric
inverse-map Hessians the curl annihilates (the brief's "Hessian
cancellation," exploited by omission). G restores those terms on the curved
path via two audited devices:

1. **Q-folding** — per-dof Hessian-channel coefficients recovered from the
   assembled E itself (`Q = M·e`, the four barycentric channels collapsing
   onto the independent parameters), which makes TET10's face-orientation
   coefficient tables unnecessary and structurally excludes the INC-243
   transcription class. Proven by Codex's probe through a negated/permuted
   face column (2.2e-16) and Grok's hand derivation of all four
   `combine_functions` cases.
2. **Constant S-vectors** — quadratic maps have constant second
   derivatives (with LIVE diagonal Voigt pairs, unlike HEX8's trilinear
   case), so the contracted `mCurv` tensors are link-time constants,
   NaN-filled on the straight branch where the channel is structurally
   skipped (the affine map has S = 0, and `NaN·0 = NaN` would otherwise
   poison a mathematically-zero term — an audit catch).

The audit rounds killed three would-be silent defects before implementation:
`Q = mJ·e` against the member `mJ` (never written by TET10, NaN on TRI6
straight), missing signs on TRI6's unsigned partial tables, and the
per-class Jacobian-dump strides (`mW[8+2d+i]` / `mM[27+3i+d]`) whose
transposes compile fine and corrupt only the new term — each now has its
own break gate.

Test infrastructure: `EF_TestVolume` gained an appended midnode-offset
parameter — the first genuinely curved quadratic geometry in any BELFEM
test (the `aCurved` flag alone never curved anything, so the Hessian
channel was previously untestable in principle). The battery runs FD
through a Lagrange-IF-based quadratic test-side Jacobian with detJ gated at
every cluster point.
