# DR-76 Strike: STRUMPACK Refinement Stall Retired

**Date:** 2026-08-27
**Scope:** Records-only closure of DR-76 after Christian's strike ruling.
**Status:** Closed by ruling; no source edits.

## Summary

Christian ruled to strike DR-76 rather than keep it live as historical caution. The old STRUMPACK refinement-stall geometry is no longer an independent active debt; DR-88 and DR-89 carry the remaining STRUMPACK accuracy and conditioning questions.

## Evidence

- Original defect: the shared relative-tolerance default was fed into STRUMPACK iterative refinement as an unbounded stopping target, and a near-zero RHS on a converged Newton iterate could hang inside `IterativeRefinementMPI`.
- First fix, 2026-08-15: `SolverParameters` gained the explicit relative-tolerance flag and STRUMPACK gained `maxit = 50`.
- Later design, 2026-08-18: the have-flag gate was retired after Christian's reltol A/B showed that the loose STRUMPACK exit test was itself the printed nonlinear residual and drove timestep collapse.
- Current code path: STRUMPACK `auto` maps to `PREC_GMRES`, unmapped Krylov methods hard-error, reltol 1e-10 and abstol 1e-14 are always applied, and the maxit cap remains as a cost bound.
- Successor ownership: DR-88 tracks the degraded-factor/conditioning question; DR-89 tracks the absolute-tolerance startup-floor gate.

- Added DR-76 to the closed archive.
