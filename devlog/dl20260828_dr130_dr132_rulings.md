## Summary

Both `[RULING]` rows were presented to Christian with fresh evidence and ruled the same session.

**DR-130 — ruled: the symmetric `h_ghost` operator is the formulation of record; the doc was stale.**
The decisive new evidence came from git, not from another derivation: commit `8fd48922`
(2026-04-07) added the adjoint-consistency terms to all four blocks, and the devlog it ships —
`dl20260406_symmetric_nitsche_audit.md` — opens by describing the change as "intended to make the
ghost/Nitsche matrix symmetric". The March 2026 nonsymmetric variant (consistency terms only,
per `dl20260319_ghost_thinshell.md`) was therefore replaced deliberately, and the resulting
symmetric-with-harmonic-weighting scheme is the SWIP method of Ern, Stephansen & Zunino 2009.
The "kernel accidentally symmetrized, doc names the lost intent" horn is refuted.
`ghost_penalty_stabilization.md` (created 2026-06-20) had carried the March narrative forward
past the April change. A supporting fact: `IWG_Maxwell` advertises `SymmetryMode::Unsymmetric`
(`cl_IWG_Maxwell.cpp:39`), so the local ghost-block symmetry never made a solver claim either way.

**DR-132 — ruled: documentation-only contract, no version handshake for 1.0.**
"Rebuild your plugin when BELFEM's headers change" stays a documented contract at the `dlopen`
boundary (`UserMaterialTemplate.cmake`, the "no version handshake" paragraph). If enforcement is
ever wanted post-release (a packed abi symbol checked after `dlopen`), that is a new register row.
No code changed for this ruling.
