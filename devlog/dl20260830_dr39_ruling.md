## What DR-39 was, and what it is now

Three items were filed; (c) `.subckt` was discharged 2026-08-27 when the netlist parser shipped.
Two survived. They are different kinds of thing and were being carried as one `[MIXED]` row.

**(a) — duplicate circuit Newton loop.** Confirmed present: `Controller::solve_circuit()`
(`cl_FEM_Controller.cpp:2528-2585`) and `electricalCircuit.cpp:134-164`. No `CircuitSolver` exists
anywhere in `src/` or `tests/`.

**RULED 2026-08-30 (Christian): the extraction is refuted; (a) discharges by deleting the demo.**
The two copies are the same ~15-line loop, differ only in where their constants come from
(`beta=1.1, alpha=0.5, gamma=0.4, 100 it, 1e-6` hardcoded vs deck-driven) and which box they print,
and the one difference that ever looked numerical — `tEpsilon0_Circuit` at the loop TOP in the
controller (`:2546`) and the BOTTOM in the executable (`:162`) — is structural: both compare the
current residual against the same previous value, and `residual()` holds no state
(`norm(mRHS)/mRHSnorm`). A helper shared between one production caller and one hardcoded demo buys
a seam, not safety.

**(b) — `Pulse`/`PWL`.** Still absent from `SourceFunctionType` (`cl_SourceFunction.hpp:57-68`);
the netlist parser names both and refuses them loudly until Phase 6
(`cl_NgspiceCircuitFactory.hpp:62`, `.cpp:430-436`). Now the whole of DR-39.

## The plan had been overtaken three times

`todo/circuit_demo_harvest_plan.md` (2026-08-28, audited) is the right remedy and was adopted —
but three of its premises expired in the two days since, all in the same direction: work landed.

1. **DR-118 was fixed *in place* on 2026-08-29, not deleted.** The executable's loop is now
   controller parity (`:113-193`). §1's "Duplicate Newton loop, **and a broken one**" row is struck,
   and R9 no longer discharges DR-118 — that already happened, on DR-118's own forced-rejection gate.
2. **R1 and R3 are already satisfied in-tree.** `tests/circuit/test_ElectricalCircuit.cpp:56-81`
   carries the fixed-Δt driver (`solve_attempt`/`take_step`) and `:84-99, 535+` the series-RLC
   circuit (`create_rlc_circuit`, `RLCRingAcrossRejectedStep`). So the harvest goes into that
   **existing** TU, not the proposed new `test_TransientCircuits.cpp` — a second TU would duplicate
   the driver, which is a poor look on a de-duplication row. R1 is superseded by **R1′**, whose
   only real content is adding the ω relaxation update to `solve_attempt` behind a defaulted
   parameter (existing callers keep ω ≡ 1 bit-for-bit; R5 needs the relaxation or four exponentials
   from a cold start overflow the Newton).
3. **O3 resolved by events.** DR-123 was fixed and struck 2026-08-29 and the rejected-step switch
   case is now a green test, so the plan's "would land a red test against an unfixed defect" no
   longer applies. §2's DR-118/DR-123 justifications for fixed Δt are struck; fixed Δt stays on its
   own merits.

**The deletion case is unweakened, and rests on what always carried it.** The binary takes no input
file and no arguments beyond `-v`, writes `circuitAnalysis.txt` with hardcoded node and component
indices, can solve only Frederic's circuit — and ships installed (`config/globals.cmake:14`). Three
of its four circuits are not merely stale but unbuildable: `rg -w 'SourceType' src/` returns
**only** those three commented lines (`:69,76,82`); the enum exists nowhere in the tree.

**The counter-case was considered and lost.** The netlist front-end shipping on 2026-08-27 makes a
file-driven runner cheap, and the "run a circuit without FEM" need is real. But that is a feature to
be specified and funded (parser plan Phases 4-5), and keeping the stub as its placeholder ships the
placeholder instead of the feature — on freeze day.

## DR-39(b): three constraints, written into the row so they are not rediscovered

- **It is not circuit-scoped.** `boundary_condition_function_type()` is the deck-string mapper for
  `cl_MaxwellBoundaryConditionFactory.cpp:491` and `cl_ThermalBoundaryConditionFactory.cpp:300` as
  well as `cl_ElectricalCircuitFactory.cpp:284`. Adding the types widens the accepted `type :` value
  set for **every** boundary condition — an Input Contract change in both artifacts. Additive, so
  `[P]` holds.
- **The storage does not fit.** `mValues` is a fixed 7-slot `Cell< real >` and `synch()` is
  `broadcast( mValues )` (`cl_SourceFunction.cpp:81-104, 238-241`). PULSE's seven parameters fit only
  by adding slots; **PWL does not fit at all** — a variable-length breakpoint list is exactly the
  payload the MPI rule routes through `share`/`receive` and never `broadcast`.
- **PWL storage must be `Cell< real >`.** The header forbids including `cl_Vector.hpp`/`cl_Matrix.hpp`
  on user-material plugin ABI grounds (`cl_SourceFunction.hpp:43-44`).
