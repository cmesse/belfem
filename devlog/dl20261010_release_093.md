# BELFEM 0.9.3: interface and thin-shell corrections

**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Purpose:** Record how 0.9.3 was cut from `devel`, what gated it, and what the gates left open.
**Module:** release packaging (`CITATION.cff`, `CHANGELOG.md`); the version line in `CMakeLists.txt` already read 0.9.3

## What happened

`devel` stood at `f95bb8c`, 174 commits ahead of `main` (`ef6d47f2`, v0.9.2), with no commit on
`main` that `devel` lacked, so the merge is a fast-forward. The release was first attempted on
2026-10-08 and held for two days while late changes were audited, three of them were taken out
again, and the gates were rerun on the final tip.

Release commit on top of `devel`: `CITATION.cff` 0.9.2 -> 0.9.3 (`date-released` 2026-10-10),
`CHANGELOG.md` `[Unreleased]` -> `[0.9.3]`, and this entry. `CMakeLists.txt` carried
`VERSION 0.9.3` since the pre-check of 2026-10-04 and is not touched.

What landed between the first attempt and the release, in order: the shared conductor-side
interface copy (DR-198, `c1d2bf7`); the `~CutFactory` double delete and the thin-shell
node-facet tables (DR-197/194, `f681f98`); the terminal-loop closure (DR-122, `0e1e913`); the
periodic-pair refusal messages (`00b7972`); `examples/pancake_tape` removed (`f6618b9`); the
automatic gauge pin beside a symmetry plane (`12a8eec`); DR-196 struck (`c31ea43`); the
mixed-master-type sideset refusal and id-ordered interface copies (`cfaf7e8`); the `gauge`
boundary condition (DR-199, `f8ce949`); `examples/corc_twolayer` back to two turns per period
(`6cb9685`); `Chain` keys as `index_t` and snake_case accessors (DR-177, `202e62a`, `0579d3a`,
`7011f44`); cut copies appended in ascending copy id (`6d5c56c`); the simplex-count accessors
and two counters outside the core as `index_t` (`6e2e9d8`, `00eebb6`). The tip `f95bb8c` is a
merge of Christian's local `devel` into the remote one; its tree differs from `00eebb6` only by
the devlog `dl20261009_stoyan_enclosed_slit_tape.md` and its index line, so the gates run on
`00eebb6` cover the released code.

Taken out again on Christian's ruling of 2026-10-09: the first-facet sideset campaign
(`e30bd98`, `411e69a`, reverted by `99f8a8f`) and an unpushed change to
`Topology::detect_sideset_types`. Reason: `GmshReader::create_sidesets` builds one sideset per
geometric entity, so a mesh written by Gmsh cannot contain the mixed sidesets those changes
guarded against, and the Topology change was not inert (it changed the cut-pruning exemption of
a boundary sideset). The work is kept on the branch `archive/first-facet-sidesets` (`93d8318`).

## Open

Findings from the audits and gates that were filed, not fixed:

- **Unexplained CI failure, once.** Server pipeline 65, job 244 (DEBUG/ARMADILLO on `7011f44`):
  `test_fem`, `FacetSlavePoints.TRI3` and `.TRI6` threw `std::bad_alloc`, `.QUAD4` then
  segfaulted. Not reproduced: the retry of the same job, pipeline 66 on the tip, 300 repetitions
  of the suite in a CI-identical build on the server (peak 41 MB resident, passes down to a
  768 MB address-space limit), and on the workstation valgrind memcheck (0 errors), 50 shuffled
  and 20 plain repetitions. Nothing the test uses changed since the last green run. No
  out-of-memory kill is logged; the server has 7.6 GB of RAM and was loaded. Cause not
  established. Valgrind ran on the gcc 11.5 build only; the server has none.
- **Numbering is not platform-independent.** `mesh::symrcm` sorts neighbours by degree with
  `std::sort` and no tie-break (`fn_Graph_symrcm.cpp`, `op_Graph_Vertex_Degree.hpp`); it runs
  before the cohomology, on the enriched mesh before the `.bfm` is written, and on the dof
  graph. An id tie-break is the remaining step.
- **INT64.** In `src/homology`, simplex keys and mesh-scale counts are 64-bit; generator, cut
  and sideset counts, the Smith machinery and the `createMatrixFrom*Map` ordinals remain 32-bit
  (census in `devlog/dl20261009_dr177_int64_completion.md`). Compile-only: there is no 64-bit
  stack to run. Outside `src/homology`, 63 compile errors in 14 files remain under
  `USE_MKL_64BIT_API=ON`.
- **`CutProcessor` constructor on a refusal.** The non-unit-coefficient refusal in
  `CutData::collect_coefficients` throws inside the `CutProcessor` constructor, which leaks its
  `CutData` objects and leaves edge flags and indices modified in throwing builds.
- **Allocation-failure leaks in `CutFactory`:** the enriched-interface sideset at
  `restore_thin_shell_sidesets` and two sites in `duplicate_and_relink_facets`.
- **`InterfaceProcessor`:** copies allocated before a periodic refusal are not owned by anyone
  (pre-existing); a sideset is classified from its first facet, as in 0.9.2 (cannot bite on a
  Gmsh mesh); `to_string( DomainType )` prints both coatings as "Coating".
- **`Curve::length()`** reads the second-to-last arclength sample on a closed curve (no caller);
  `Curve::reverse` does not rebase the arclength; `close_terminal_loop` is not idempotent.
- **`BeltedTree.cpp:332`** looks up a mesh element by id with a cochain index (no caller).
- **`cl_GM_EoS_Hydrogen.hpp:52`** declares `mNab` as `real` and uses it as a size.
- **`examples/rutherford`:** `README.md` describes REBCO tapes, `input.conf` ships BSCCO-2223.
- **`examples/pancake_tape`** (removed): the first solve has three φ null pivots on element-less
  original nodes, two of them where the two cut sheets coincide over 66 triangles
  (`devlog/dl20261009_pancake_tape_run_and_removal.md`). For Gregory: is it expected that two
  generators' cuts share faces?
- **`examples/corc_twolayer` at 4/3 turns:** chained tapes give a generator with no unit
  representative; the old mesh is a 42-second reproducer
  (`devlog/dl20261009_corc_twolayer_numturns.md`). For Gregory.
- **For Gregory, unanswered:** DR-71 (the unused branch without suggested homologies and the
  unbounded search in `pGeneralizedCombine`) and DR-29 (the coreduction proposal).
- **Tests the auditors found weak:** `test_CutFactory_unwind.cpp` has no counter and never calls
  the restore step; the two numbering tests cannot force a different hash order.
