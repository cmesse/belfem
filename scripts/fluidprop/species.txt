# --- parser coverage ---
# These earn their place by shape rather than by use. A composition line holds up to
# five element/count pairs, and no species above fills more than three: BrF3 has two
# elements with a high multiplicity, and the two halons have four each. They keep the
# fixed column reader under test on records the rest of the table never produces.

# --- reference elements for the formation table. Gas::create_reference_gases
# --- anchors formation enthalpies on the standard state of each element; for
# --- the elements above that is the molecule gas itself, except carbon
# --- ( graphite ) and bromine ( liquid, which is the CEA reference state at
# --- 298.15 K ). Cl2 covers the chlorinated species. Condensed records carry
# --- no transport data and get no generated interval, which is expected.

Cl2
