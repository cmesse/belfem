## 2. Architecture: one sentence at the declaration

Each contract is one sentence in the header a caller includes, in the form the guideline's §5 examples use. For the `send`/`receive`/`share`/`distribute` families the sentence sits on the scalar overload and every other overload of the same name gets `/** Same contract as send( T ). */`; the tag-protocol reason (L01) is stated once at `comm_tag()` in `commtools.cpp` and pointed at. For `cl_Matrix.hpp`/`cl_Vector.hpp` the file-level contract goes into the dispatch header, since that is what a caller includes, and the backend `data()` accessors point at it. Nothing is copied into `.cpp` files; the three-copies-drift problem the guideline records is the reason.
